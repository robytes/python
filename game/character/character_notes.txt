CHARACTER
│
├── Immutable Base Attributes (roll 3d6, rolling 1s until no 1s, and multiply by 5)
│   ├── Fitness
│   ├── Intellect
│   ├── Influence # represents what they can deliberately accomplish socially—persuasion, deception, intimidation, negotiation, etc.
│   └── Spirit
│
├── Derived Stats
│   ├── Presence # represents the immediate impression a character makes: appearance, bearing, confidence, magnetism
│   │    └── Fitness + Influence
│   ├── Resolve # represents resistance to psychological stress.
│   │    └── Spirit + Intellect
│   └── Luck
│
├── Mutable Resources
│   ├── HP
│   ├── MP
│   └── Composure # resource that can actually be depleted by frightening or psychologically damaging experiences
│
├── Reputation
│   └── Established during character creation,
│       then modified by events in the world, Character creation establishes it, and actions during the game modify it. Eventually it could vary by faction, settlement, organization, NPC, etc.
│
├── Inventory
│
└── Equipment
    ├── Weapon
    └── Armor
